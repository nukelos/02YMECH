\section*{Question 1}
Consider two particles moving with velocities described by vectors $\vec v_{1}~=~\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{m/s}$ and $~\vec v_{2}~=~\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{m/s.}$ At time $t = 0$~s, they were located at points $\vec r_{10}~=~\begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$~m and $\vec r_{20}~=~\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}$~m. Determine the vector of their relative position as a function of time, and calculate time and distance when they are closest to each other.
\section*{Question 2}
You are throwing a ball into the wind, and the wind is blowing in the same direction as the throw of the ball. The wind exerts a constant horizontal acceleration on the ball, represented as $a=\frac{g}{\sqrt{3}}$, where $g$ is the gravitational acceleration. This acceleration acts in the direction of the ball's motion along the x-axis. You initially launch the ball with a velocity of $v_0$. Your goal is to find the launch angle $\alpha$ that will allow the ball to travel the farthest horizontal distance possible

\section*{Question 3}
A projectile with a mass $m$ strikes a wall with a velocity $v_1$ and exits from the other side with a velocity $v_2$ after penetrating the wall of thickness $d$. What was the average force $F$ acting on the projectile inside the wall? Use your knowledge of dynamics!

\section*{Question 4}
A mathematical pendulum with a length of $l$ is attached to the arm of a rotating carousel at a distance $d$ from its center. The pendulum is deflected from its vertical position by an angle $\alpha$. Calculate the period of rotation for the carousel.
